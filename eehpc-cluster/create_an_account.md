# How to Create an EEHPC Account

To obtain an **EEHPC Cluster account**, please follow the steps below:

1. **Confirm LEAP Lab Membership**  
   Ensure that you are an official member of **LEAP Lab**.

2. **Send an Email Request**  
   Drop an email to the **EEHPC Cluster responsible person** listed on the [LEAP Lab Logistics homepage](https://iiscleap.github.io/),  
   keeping **Sriram** (`sriramg@iisc.ac.in`) in **CC**.

3. **Fill Out the Registration Form**  
   Complete the Google registration form here: [EEHPC Account Request Form](https://forms.gle/aNdH2uTodcia81ky5).

---

Once both steps are completed, your **EEHPC account** will be created.  
The **login credentials** will be communicated to you **via email**.
