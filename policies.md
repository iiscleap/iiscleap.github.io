# Cluster Usage Policies

Please follow these rules while using the LEAP cluster:

- 🚫 **Do not run heavy jobs on the login node.** Always use the job scheduler.
- ✅ **Use appropriate queues** for short, medium, and long GPU jobs.
- 🗃️ **Store large datasets in `/data/`**, not in your home directory.
- 🧹 **Clean up `/scratch/` regularly.** It is auto-cleaned every 30 days.
- 🔐 **Do not share your account**. One user = one login.
- 🛠️ **Software requests** should be sent to `cluster-admin@leap.iisc.ac.in`.

Non-compliance may lead to account suspension.
